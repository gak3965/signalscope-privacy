SignalScope — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Who we are

SignalScope (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is operated by the SignalScope developer. For any privacy question, contact Gfunk3965@gmail.com.

What the app does

SignalScope scans the device’s surroundings for wireless signals that the operating system makes available — WiFi networks, Bluetooth LE devices, and cellular connection details — and displays their public metadata (names, signal strength, channel, band, identifiers) so you can analyze your local radio environment.

The app does not decode, record, or access the content of any transmission. It reads only the presence and technical metadata the OS exposes to apps.

Location permission — important

Android and iOS require location permission before an app may read WiFi or Bluetooth scan results. SignalScope requests location solely to enable scanning. The app does not track, store, or transmit your location, and does not use it for advertising or analytics.

How data is used and stored

  • All scan data, history, favorites and survey data are processed and stored on your device only.
  • Saved scans and tracked devices persist locally until you delete them in the app.
  • We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties.

Outbound requests you initiate

A few optional tools make outbound internet requests only when you tap them. They send no personal data beyond what any web request includes:

  • Public IP / ISP lookup uses ipinfo.io to report your public IP and ISP.
  • Speed test downloads from Cloudflare’s speed endpoint to measure throughput.
  • Map tiles for the coverage heatmap are loaded from OpenStreetMap.
  • IP geolocation (optional, in the packet analyzer) sends public IP addresses from an opened capture to ip-api.com to show their country/city.

Exports (CSV/JSON/PDF/KML/GeoJSON) are handed to your device’s own share sheet; where they go from there is your choice.

Data we collect about you

We collect no personal data and use no third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. Nothing is transmitted to us — we operate no servers that receive your data.

Permissions summary

  • Location (fine) — required by the OS for WiFi/Bluetooth scanning, and to tag coverage surveys.
  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth — to detect Bluetooth LE devices and trackers.
  • Phone state — to read cellular network type and signal (Android).
  • Camera — only for the AR signal finder’s viewfinder overlay.
  • NFC — only when you start an NFC tag scan.
  • Notifications — to alert you about tracked devices and connection changes.

You can revoke any permission in your device settings; the related scanning feature will stop working.

Children

SignalScope is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction) and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes
